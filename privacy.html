Privacy

How lateframe handles your information — in plain language.

Last updated: 6 October 2026 · Operated by Devansh Sharma

01Who we are

lateframe ("lateframe", "we", "us") is a video editing studio run by Devansh Sharma, who is responsible for how your personal information is handled on this website. You can reach him at hello@example.com.

02What this website collects

This is a portfolio website. It has no accounts, no sign-up and no payment forms, and it does not run advertising or analytics trackers. We do not set tracking cookies.

We only receive personal information that you choose to send us when you contact us — by WhatsApp, email, phone or the project form. That usually means:

  • your name and contact details (email address, phone number, WhatsApp number);
  • what you tell us about your project, and any links to footage, references or files you share;
  • anything else you decide to include in your message.

03How we use it

We use what you send us to reply to you, discuss and quote your project, deliver the work, and keep a record of what was agreed. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

04Third-party services

Some ways of contacting us run on other companies' services, which have their own privacy policies and handle your data under their own terms:

  • WhatsApp (Meta) — when you message us on WhatsApp;
  • Google Forms (Google) — when you submit our project form;
  • Instagram, YouTube, Vimeo and X — if you follow a link to our profiles or reels. Reels on this site are only loaded from Instagram, YouTube or Vimeo after you press play, and those services then handle your data under their own policies.

Fonts and images on this website are served from the site itself, not from a third-party font or tracking service.

05Your footage and files

Footage, music, photos or other material you send us for editing stays yours. We use it only to do the work you asked for, we do not share it with anyone outside the project, and we delete working files within a reasonable time after the project is delivered unless you ask us to keep them or the project is ongoing.

We may show finished edits in our portfolio or on social media, but only with your permission when the work is a private or client project.

06How long we keep information

Messages and project details are kept for as long as needed to complete the project and to keep a sensible record of the engagement (for example, for invoices or follow-up work), and then deleted or archived securely.

07Your choices

You can ask us at any time to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Write to hello@example.com and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can. Where the law applicable to you (including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023) gives you further rights, we will respect them.

08Children

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us information, tell us and we will delete it.

09Security

We take reasonable care to protect the information you send us, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information we will update this page and the date above. Continuing to use the website or to contact us after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact Devansh Sharma, lateframe, at hello@example.com.
